Welcome to Geeta University

Geeta University is a premier educational institution committed to academic excellence and holistic development. Established with a vision to nurture future leaders, we offer world-class education across diverse disciplines including Engineering, Management, Law, and Humanities. Our 100-acre lush green campus provides the perfect environment for learning and growth, equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, modern laboratories, and a well-stocked library. With highly qualified faculty and industry-aligned curriculum, we prepare students to excel in their chosen fields. Geeta University takes pride in its outstanding placement records, research contributions, and vibrant campus life that fosters innovation, creativity, and social responsibility.
